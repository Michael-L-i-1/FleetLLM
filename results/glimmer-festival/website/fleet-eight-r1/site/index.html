Lantern Commons Arts Weekend

12–13 June 2027 • Alder Park, Brookfield

A warm, welcoming weekend of music, making and community under paper lanterns in Alder Park. Join neighbours, artists and families for live performances, hands-on workshops and shared stories.

Under-12s free with an adult. No booking fees. Family hours 10:00–16:00.
Family highlights: Family Story Circle and Clay Play are perfect for all ages with hands-on making and gentle storytelling.
