Festival questions

Under-12s free with an adult. No booking fees. Family hours 10:00–16:00.
Are families welcome?

All ages are welcome. Under-12s free with an adult. Family Story Circle and Clay Play offer activities for children and caregivers.

What happens in the rain?

Events continue in covered areas. Updates are posted if severe weather requires changes.

Can we bring food?

Picnics are welcome. Please avoid glass containers.

How do I arrive?

Enter via the Riverpath entrance. Bicycle parking is available near Workshop Yard.
