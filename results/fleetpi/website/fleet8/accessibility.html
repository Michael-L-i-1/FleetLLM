Accessibility and welcome guide

Family advisory: Under-12s free with an adult. No booking fees. Family hours 10:00–16:00.

Lantern Commons Arts Weekend is designed to be welcoming for all visitors. The festival takes place at Alder Park, Brookfield, 18 River Walk, Brookfield, on 12–13 June 2027. We provide step-free routes, a quiet space, and accessible toilets across the site.

Step-free routes

Main paths between River Stage, Workshop Yard and Story Grove are paved and step-free. Entrances at Riverpath entrance are level with no stairs. Portable ramps are available at Workshop Yard for any raised platforms.

Quiet space

A designated quiet space is located beside Story Grove, near the shaded storytelling lawn. The area offers low sound levels, seating, and a calm environment for visitors who need a break from busy areas.

Family-friendly note: The quiet space is especially welcoming during family hours starting at 10:00. Families with young children can use the calm area for rest and sensory breaks throughout the day, with step-free access from the main path.

Accessible toilets

Accessible toilets are available at Workshop Yard and near River Stage. Facilities include wide doors, grab rails, and clear signage. Staff can assist with access if needed.

Access support
If you need specific arrangements, please contact us in advance at access@lanterncommons.example. We are happy to discuss step-free routes, quiet space, accessible toilets, and other support.
