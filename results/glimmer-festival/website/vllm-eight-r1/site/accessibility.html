Access & Inclusion Guide

Lantern Commons Arts Weekend is welcoming for all visitors. We’re working with Alder Park, Brookfield to make 12–13 June 2027 comfortable and enjoyable. If you need specific support, email access@lanterncommons.example and we’ll plan with you.

Family-friendly quiet space

The designated quiet space beside Story Grove is a calm, shaded area away from amplified sound. It’s especially helpful during family hours starting at 10:00, with low-stimulus seating, water and a staff member on hand. Families can take a break anytime and return to activities at their own pace.

Step-free routes

Main entrances at Riverpath entrance are step-free with firm, level paths to River Stage, Workshop Yard and Story Grove. Temporary ramps are provided where thresholds occur. The park’s street address is 18 River Walk, Brookfield.

Quiet space

A designated quiet space is available beside Story Grove, shaded and away from amplified sound. Low-stimulus seating, water and a staff member are on hand during performances. You can take a break anytime.

Accessible toilets

Accessible toilets are located near Workshop Yard and by the River Stage entrance. Changing facilities are available on request. Please contact us in advance for assistance.
