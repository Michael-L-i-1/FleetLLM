Our Mission

Common Ground Coffee is a nonprofit cafe in Palo Alto where great coffee funds real opportunity. We bring neighbors together over a good cup, and we use every surplus to build pathways out of poverty.

We are a nonprofit. 100% of surplus funds go to job training — hands-on barista apprenticeships, resume coaching, and paid placements for people from under-resourced communities. No certifications invented, just honest work, shared tables, and a commitment to keep the doors open for everyone.
