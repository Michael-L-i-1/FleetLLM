Festival Venues at Alder Park

Family notice: Under-12s free with an adult. No booking fees. Family hours 10:00–16:00.

Three welcoming spaces across Alder Park, Brookfield, for music, making and storytelling. All venues are within easy walking distance of the Riverpath entrance.

Family tip: Story Grove offers a shaded storytelling lawn beside the Quiet space, ideal for little ones to settle.
Alder Park address: 18 River Walk, Brookfield. See access guide for step-free routes, quiet space and accessible toilets.

River Stage

Main outdoor music stage beside the river path.

Workshop Yard

Covered making tables, water points and bicycle parking.

Story Grove

Shaded storytelling lawn beside the quiet space.
