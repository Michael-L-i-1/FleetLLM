Volunteer with us

Family-friendly policies: Under-12s free with an adult. No booking fees. Family hours 10:00–16:00.

Help make Lantern Commons Arts Weekend warm and welcoming for everyone. We need friendly hands for 12–13 June 2027 at Alder Park, Brookfield. No experience needed, just enthusiasm and a smile.

Welcome crew

Greet visitors at the Riverpath entrance, hand out programmes, and share the day’s highlights. Great for people who love conversation and wayfinding.

Workshop helpers

Support making activities in Workshop Yard, assist with materials, and help keep spaces tidy and safe. Ideal if you enjoy hands-on community making.

Family welcome

Special family-focused support 10:00–13:00 on Saturday. Help families find quiet space, accessible routes, and family-friendly activities.

Family welcome shift runs 10:00–13:00 to support families with young children during peak family hours.
