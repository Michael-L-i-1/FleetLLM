Accessible Welcome Guide

Under-12s free with an adult. No booking fees. Family hours 10:00–16:00.

Lantern Commons Arts Weekend is committed to welcoming everyone. Please get in touch to plan your visit.

Step-free routes

All main entrances and key areas are step-free. The Riverpath entrance provides level access to River Stage and Workshop Yard. Temporary ramps are available at Story Grove where the path slopes gently.

Quiet space

A designated quiet space is available beside Story Grove for visitors who need a calmer environment. The area is low-volume, with seating and clear signage. Staff can direct you to the space at any time.

Family-friendly quiet support

The quiet space is especially helpful during Family hours starting at 10:00. Families can use the low-volume area beside Story Grove for a calm break, with seating and clear signage. Staff are available to guide you.

Accessible toilets

Accessible toilets are located near Workshop Yard and by the River Stage entrance. Changing facilities are available on request. Please contact the access team in advance for specific needs.
