Access information

Under-12s free with an adult. No booking fees. Family hours 10:00–16:00.

We welcome all visitors. Please contact us to plan your visit.

Quiet space beside Story Grove links with the 10:00 start of family hours, offering a calm area for families during the day.

Step-free routes

Main paths around River Stage, Workshop Yard and Story Grove are step-free with firm surfaces. Accessible parking is available near the Riverpath entrance.

Quiet space

A designated quiet area is available beside Story Grove for visitors who need a calmer environment.

Accessible toilets

Accessible toilets are located at Workshop Yard and near River Stage.
