Our Mission

Common Ground Coffee is a nonprofit. We exist to make excellent coffee a catalyst for community, not profit.

We are a nonprofit organization, and 100% of surplus funds support job training for neighbors seeking pathways into hospitality careers. Every cup helps fund hands-on learning, mentorship, and paid work experience.

We brew with care, source responsibly, and welcome everyone at the table. Our kitchen and café are a training ground where people build skills, confidence, and connections that last beyond a single visit.
