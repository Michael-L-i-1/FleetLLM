Accessibility Guide for Lantern Commons

Family policies: Under-12s free with an adult. No booking fees. Family hours 10:00–16:00.

We want everyone to enjoy the weekend comfortably. Please get in touch early so we can support your visit.

Step-free routes

Main paths around Alder Park are paved and step-free. River Stage, Workshop Yard and Story Grove are reachable via gentle gradients with no stairs. Portable ramps are available at entrances on request.

Quiet space

A low-stimulus quiet area is set up near Story Grove. It offers seating, dimmed lighting and a break from music. Staff can guide you to the space and provide a quiet signal card.

Family-friendly tip: The quiet space is especially welcoming during family hours starting at 10:00, offering a calm break for children and adults alike.

Accessible toilets

Accessible toilets are located at Workshop Yard and near River Stage. They are step-free, with grab rails and ample turning space. Changing facilities are available on request.
