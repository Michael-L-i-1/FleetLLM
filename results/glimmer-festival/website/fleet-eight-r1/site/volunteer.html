Volunteer with Lantern Commons

Under-12s free with an adult. No booking fees. Family hours 10:00–16:00.

Join the Welcome crew and Workshop helpers for Lantern Commons Arts Weekend on 12–13 June 2027 at Alder Park, Brookfield. Help make the festival warm and welcoming for all visitors.

Family welcome shift runs 10:00–13:00 to support families arriving early. Help with welcome, signage and family-friendly guidance.

Welcome crew

Greet visitors at the Riverpath entrance, hand out programmes, and share information about step-free routes and quiet spaces.

Workshop helpers

Support making activities in Workshop Yard, assist with materials, and help keep the covered tables tidy and accessible.

Family welcome

Special 10:00–13:00 support for families with young children, helping with arrival, quiet space info and family activities.
