Frequently Asked Questions

Under-12s free with an adult. No booking fees. Family hours 10:00–16:00.

Everything you need to know for Lantern Commons Arts Weekend, 12–13 June 2027 at Alder Park, Brookfield.

Are families and children welcome?

All ages are welcome. Under-12s free with an adult. The weekend includes family-friendly activities and a relaxed atmosphere throughout Alder Park.

What happens in the rain?

Events continue in covered areas. Updates will be shared if severe weather requires changes.

Can we bring food?

Picnics are welcome. Please avoid glass containers to keep the park safe and tidy.

How do I arrive and park?

Arrival is via Riverpath entrance. Bicycle parking is available near Workshop Yard.
