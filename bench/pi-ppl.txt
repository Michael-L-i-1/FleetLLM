A rainbow forms when sunlight enters a water droplet and bends. Different wavelengths bend by different amounts. Some light reflects inside the droplet, then bends again as it exits. Many droplets send different colors toward the observer, producing an arc. The Sun must be behind the observer and the droplets ahead.
