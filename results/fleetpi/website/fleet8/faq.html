Frequently Asked Questions

Family advisory: Under-12s free with an adult. No booking fees. Family hours 10:00–16:00.

Quick answers for Lantern Commons Arts Weekend, 12–13 June 2027 at Alder Park, Brookfield.

Are families welcome?

All ages are welcome. Under-12s free with an adult. The weekend is designed for families with gentle activities, storytelling and hands-on making.

What happens if it rains?

Events continue in covered areas. The Workshop Yard is covered, and we monitor weather. Severe weather may require updates, which will be posted on site.

Can we bring food?

Picnics are welcome. Please avoid glass containers. Water points are available at Workshop Yard.

How do I arrive?

Arrival is via Riverpath entrance. Bicycle parking is available near Workshop Yard. The park address is 18 River Walk, Brookfield.
