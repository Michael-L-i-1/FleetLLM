Volunteer with us

Under-12s free with an adult. No booking fees. Family hours 10:00–16:00.

Help make Lantern Commons Arts Weekend warm and welcoming. Join a small team supporting artists, visitors and the park on 12–13 June 2027 at Alder Park, Brookfield.

Welcome crew

Greet visitors at the Riverpath entrance, hand out programmes, and share information about the festival layout and facilities.

Workshop helpers

Support making activities in Workshop Yard, assist with materials, set up tables, and help keep the space tidy and safe.

Family welcome

Family welcome shift 10:00–13:00. Meet families at the entrance, share family-friendly activities, and support quiet space guidance.
