Our mission

Common Ground Coffee is a nonprofit cafe rooted in Palo Alto. We believe great coffee can build lasting community.

We operate as a nonprofit and commit that 100% of surplus funds support job training for neighbors seeking pathways into hospitality careers. Every cup helps fund hands-on learning, mentorship, and paid work experience.

With warmth, dignity, and care, we create a welcoming space where people gather, learn, and grow together.
