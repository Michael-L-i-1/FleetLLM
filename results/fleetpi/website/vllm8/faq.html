Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for Lantern Commons Arts Weekend, 12–13 June 2027 at Alder Park, Brookfield.

Family advisory: Under-12s free with an adult. No booking fees. Family hours 10:00–16:00.
Is the festival family friendly?

All ages are welcome. Under-12s free with an adult. The programme includes gentle storytelling, hands-on making and relaxed music, with family activities designed for children and caregivers to enjoy together.

What happens in the rain?

Events continue in covered areas where possible. The Workshop Yard and parts of Story Grove are sheltered, and we monitor weather closely. If severe weather requires updates, notices will be posted on site and via the festival channels.

Can we bring food?

Picnics are welcome. Please avoid glass containers and take all rubbish home. Water points are available at Workshop Yard, and local vendors will be on site.

How do I arrive and park a bike?

Arrival is via the Riverpath entrance to Alder Park. Bicycle parking is provided near Workshop Yard, and step-free routes connect the main areas.
